Variation (seed) — the same seed always builds the exact same mission (that's how a share link reproduces it precisely). Keep it to fine-tune the same layout; change it or hit to re-roll a different spread of parked aircraft, threats and support.
Your loadout
Your jet is fitted for the mission type you picked — air-to-air gets missiles, strike gets bombs and a targeting pod, SEAD gets anti-radiation missiles, and every fit keeps a pair of air-to-air missiles for self-defense. Everything chosen is checked against what DCS actually allows on that station and against the year, so nothing turns up that the aircraft can't carry or that hadn't been invented yet. Untick to spawn with empty pylons and set it yourself in the Mission Editor.

3Airfields

Fill your side's ramps with parked aircraft, ground equipment and infrastructure.

AI aircraft use DCS parking orientation and more memory/CPU. Static objects use exact surveyed headings and estimates elsewhere. The Channel, South Atlantic and Iraq have no surveyed headings yet. Static is recommended for lower memory use.
Parked statics currently wear DCS stock skins. The curated nation-correct pack ships unverified — its livery names are guesses, and a name DCS doesn't recognise gives you a wrong or blank aircraft rather than the default one, so the engine doesn't write them. Run scripts/dump_liveries.py --merge once against your install to read the real names (including paid ones) and this control takes effect.
Big aircraft need big stands, and the fighter squadrons will take every one of them if nothing holds a few back. Set this to Exercise surge for a Red Flag look at Nellis. Small fields scale down on their own.
Give individual bases their own fill — 0% empties one, and it's the only way to populate a civilian airport.
0 aircraft per airfield. Reset to theme · Clear all
Counts are per airfield and start from the selected theme — adjust freely. Helicopters go on pads, heavies (B-1, C-130, tankers, AWACS) on large stands; anything beyond a field's capacity is skipped. Your coalition and Red/OPFOR aggressors are listed separately. Liveries use the default squadron skin (per-type picker coming next).
Only military installations are populated — civilian airports (marked in the Home list) stay undressed. Enemy fields dress automatically with their own era-correct theme. Runways and taxi routes always stay clear.

7Map graphics (F10)

The map briefs the mission: each layer draws real mission geometry on the F10 map. Friendly orbits and threat rings render on YOUR side's layer only (multiplayer-safe); bullseye and nav points are shared. Zones inform — they never route you.
