3Airfields

Fill your side's ramps with parked aircraft, ground equipment and infrastructure.

⚠ AI aircraft are live units — they use more memory and CPU and can hurt FPS on lower-end PCs. Only needed for exact facing on the two maps not yet surveyed (Falklands, The Channel). Static is recommended everywhere else.
Skins match the base's nation (a USAF F-4E won't wear USMC paint). For exact, verified liveries on YOUR install — including paid ones — run scripts/dump_liveries.py once.
Per-base overrides — set fill for individual bases
Default is "inherit" (the global fill above). Check a base to give it its own fill — 0% empties it. Civilian airports are never populated unless you deliberately override them here.
0 aircraft per airfield. Reset to theme · Clear all
Counts are per airfield and start from the selected theme — adjust freely. Helicopters go on pads, heavies (B-1, C-130, tankers, AWACS) on large stands; anything beyond a field's capacity is skipped. Your coalition and Red/OPFOR aggressors are listed separately. Liveries use the default squadron skin (per-type picker coming next).
Only military installations are populated — civilian airports (marked in the Home list) stay undressed. Enemy fields dress automatically with their own era-correct theme. Runways and taxi routes always stay clear.

7Map graphics (F10)

The map briefs the mission: each layer draws real mission geometry on the F10 map. Friendly orbits and threat rings render on YOUR side's layer only (multiplayer-safe); bullseye and nav points are shared. Zones inform — they never route you.

4Threats

Air defenses plus the Threat Dial — how hard the mission pushes back.

1 Minimal · 2 Light · 3 Moderate · 4 Heavy · 5 Maximum
On top of the SAM defending each enemy airfield, the dial adds an area SAM belt and airborne enemy CAP on the threat axis. Everything is era-gated — a WWII field never fields an SA-10. Needs Air defenses (building blocks) on.
