North star: "Select, don't search." A living, period-accurate DCS mission in under a minute — no editor, no Lua. We set the stage, you write the play. Never player waypoints.

Mission Library + two paths

Pick a curated scenario — role-coded cards, era & threat filters, preview → generate or open-in-builder — or build from scratch in the full wizard.

Each airbase dresses as its real owning nation — country, aircraft types, liveries — with per-nation ramp rosters.

Historical Airspace (first slices)
