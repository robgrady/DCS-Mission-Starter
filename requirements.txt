# TEST-ONLY, optional: `pytest-xdist`. The suite is ~3,500 tests and most of

# does:  python3 -m pytest tests -q -n auto --dist loadfile
# `--dist loadfile` matters: several modules share a module-scoped fixture that
# builds an entire mission, and the default distribution splits a module across
# workers, rebuilding that mission once per worker.
